About

Manual-QA discipline, automation-ready momentum

I'm a QA Analyst at BMM Test Lab in Dieppe, NB, where I test gaming systems against strict regulatory compliance requirements — the kind of testing where a missed edge case is a licensing problem, not just a bug ticket. My day-to-day is manual, regression, and compliance testing, backed by SQL data validation and API testing to confirm systems behave correctly beyond the UI.

I hold a B.Sc. in Systems Analysis and a Postgraduate Diploma in Quality Assurance Testing, and I'm currently extending that manual foundation into automation — already comfortable with Python and Selenium, and building Playwright as my flagship framework.
